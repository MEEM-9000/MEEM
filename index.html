MEME

Where words meet music.

Elke and Mark bring Live in which original poetic texts and music are intertwined to create an intimate, sometimes playful, sometimes melancholic storyline.

Their work explores those aspects of life that resonate with us: love and loss, memory and desire, tenderness down to the small absurdities of everyday life.
