MEME

Where words meet music.

Mark and Elke bring original poetic texts and music together in an intimate live performance that moves between tenderness, humour and melancholy.

MEME explores the experiences that resonate with us all: love and loss, memory and desire, tenderness and the small absurdities of everyday life. Words, music and performance become part of the same story.
