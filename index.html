MEEM

Where words meet music.

Elke and Mark bring a performance in which original poetic texts and music are intertwined to create an intimate, sometimes playful, sometimes melancholic storyline.

Their work explores those things that resonate with us: love and loss, memory and desire, tenderness and the small absurdities of everyday life.
