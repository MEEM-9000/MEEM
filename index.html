MEME

Where words meet music.

Mark and Elke bring a unique performance in which original poetic texts and music are intertwined to create an intimate, playful, sometimes melancholic storyline.

Their work explores those aspects of life that resonate with us all; love and loss, memory and desire, tenderness and the small absurdities of everyday life.
